Flocking

Fifty boids move across a blue canvas, steering through separation, alignment, cohesion, and attraction to a follow point. Adjust their behavior live, or click the canvas to move the target, add boids, place rocks, and introduce chasers.

Other options
  • Click inside the canvas: choose an On click action first. Follow point moves the black target (the default action); Create boid adds a green boid; Create rock adds a stationary brown obstacle; Create chaser adds a larger red boid that pursues nearby regular boids. Clicking outside the canvas does nothing to the simulation. Dragging does not continuously add objects or move the target.
  • Keyboard: there are no custom keyboard shortcuts or keyboard equivalents for canvas actions. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to reach native controls, arrow keys for sliders and radio choices, Space for checkboxes, and Enter or Space for buttons.
  • Live sliders: Separation steers away from neighbors, Alignment matches their direction, and Cohesion steers toward their center. Mouse follow attracts boids to the last clicked target, not the moving pointer. Obstacle avoidance steers away from rocks. These five weights range from 0 to 1 in steps of 0.01; 0 disables that steering contribution.
  • Neighbor distance: 0-100 px, step 1 (default 80), controls neighbor detection and the wall-avoidance zone. Speed: 0-10 px per animation frame, step 1 (default 3). The five steering defaults are 0.21, 0.5, 0.2, 0.03, and 0.2, respectively. All settings apply live to regular boids; chasers keep their original independent behavior.
  • Individual Reset: restores just that slider's HTML default and updates its output and live setting; it does not restart or remove objects.
  • Show distances: draws a circle at each regular boid's tip. The original drawing uses neighbor distance as the circle diameter, not the detection radius. Avoid walls: adds edge steering; a smaller neighbor distance can make this look better. Both options start off. Edge wrapping remains in place.
  • Restart Simulation: creates 50 new regular boids, removes all added boids, chasers, and rocks, and centers the follow target, keeping every control value. Reset Everything: restores all defaults and restarts. A native form reset has the same full-reset behavior.

The simulation targets 60 FPS on a fixed 800 by 600 logical canvas. Its display scales down uniformly on smaller screens without changing simulation coordinates. Controls stay disabled until initialization succeeds.

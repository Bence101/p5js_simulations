Train of Thought

Guide colored trains through a changing railway and send each one to a station of the same color. Every round runs automatically, so switch junctions before the trains arrive.

Goal: route all 20 trains to matching-color stations and score as many points as possible. Each correct delivery is worth 10 points.

Loading the board and sound effects. Game controls stay disabled until initialization succeeds.

Game actions
  • G or Regenerate Board: try a new layout, reset the round, and start trains automatically. If generation fails, the current game keeps running and the status explains how to retry.
  • S or Restart Game: reset score and train progress on the current board. Junction routes stay as they are.
  • Reset Everything: reset the round and return every junction to its starting exit. This game has no editable settings; its defaults are defined in init.js.
  • Full Screen: toggle the whole game page into or out of full screen.
  • Switch a junction: click a junction tile to change which of its two exits a train will follow. Clicking ordinary track or station tiles does not switch a route. Junction clicks have no keyboard equivalent; dragging is not supported.
  • Route trains: trains start automatically, one every 150 animation frames, at a speed of 0.020. A round sends 20 trains toward the eight colored stations.
  • Score: a train arriving at a station of its own color scores 10 points. A train reaching the wrong station finishes with no points. The game ends after all 20 trains finish; the HUD shows score and completed trains.
  • Sound: junction clicks, train rattling, matching-station horns, wrong-station alerts, and the finish sound play when browser audio is available. Sound is optional; the game remains playable when autoplay is blocked.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: G and S are ignored while you type in or operate a native control.
